# Frujt Global logo — local review

Recreated in SVG by referencing the business-card photograph supplied on 5 October 2026. This is a manual vector interpretation, not an extraction of original vector artwork or an exact geographic tracing.

Preserved: two broad clockwise circular arrows, their top/bottom arrangement, the central blue globe, light geographic silhouettes, dark green/near-black arrow treatment, and the Frujt Global name. The earlier field-row/globe draft is no longer used. Coastline details are simplified for small-screen legibility.

Excluded from the logo: card background, personal name, qualifications, phone number, email, and website URL. Those details are not part of the logo artwork. No contact or legal-name change was made from this reference.

Assets:
- frujt-mark-dark.svg: transparent mark, dark arrows and blue globe for light backgrounds.
- frujt-mark-light.svg: transparent mark, off-white arrows and blue globe for dark backgrounds.
- frujt-logo-dark.svg: standalone horizontal mark and wordmark for light backgrounds.
- frujt-logo-light.svg: standalone horizontal mark and wordmark for dark backgrounds.
- frujt-favicon.svg: mark on a warm-white tile for small browser icons.

Website placements: responsive header and footer use the mark with live HTML brand text; the Frujt business panel uses the dark mark; the browser favicon uses the dedicated icon. Hero text reinforces Frujt as primary and TASTAR as a connected business. TASTAR's panel has a secondary text treatment, not the Frujt mark.

Original TASTAR assets and the previous shared-mark concepts are retained on disk, but the shared-mark concepts are not referenced by the active page. The user approved deployment of this logo update on 5 October 2026.
