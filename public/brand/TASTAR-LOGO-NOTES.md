# Refined TASTAR identity

Manually redrawn SVG based on the user-supplied green leaf/lightbulb logo. Preserved the rounded bulb loop, two internal leaves, sweeping lower stem, and bulb base. Smoothed the shapes and spacing. The italic serif TASTAR name retains the original typographic direction with a flat green finish in place of raster shadows and gradients.

Assets: tastar-mark.svg, tastar-logo-dark.svg, tastar-logo-light.svg.

Website uses the mark with live serif typography in the homepage TASTAR card, TASTAR page hero, and Frujt Global page connected-business panel. Full stacked SVGs are reusable brand assets. Frujt Global remains the shared header, footer, and favicon identity. This is a visual refinement, not a legal name change.
